About

I'm on the pre-med track studying Biology at Boston University. I believe in a fair healthcare system — not as an idea I read about, but as something I've watched matter over years of serving at a hospital, supporting the Red Cross, and working with people who don't get much help.

What I keep coming back to is the work itself: communicating with people who are having a hard day, collaborating with a team, solving problems as they come, and adapting when the day doesn't go the way it was planned.
